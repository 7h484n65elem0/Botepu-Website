What non-compliance can cost you

Falling short of the Tax Administration Act has real consequences.

  • Missing out on tax refunds from SARS
  • Penalties levied by SARS
  • Being unable to access your retirement funds
  • Being unable to borrow money in the form of a mortgage

About Botepu

Botepu is a registered tax practitioner practice based in Amberfield City, Centurion. We act as the link between you and SARS, so your returns are filed correctly and on time, your information stays up to date, and disputes get resolved.

We are a team of qualified tax practitioners and professionals dedicated to providing personalised, efficient, and accurate tax compliance services. Our mission is to simplify the complexities of SARS submissions for individuals and businesses, helping you save time, maximize deductions, and navigate tax seasons with complete confidence.

At Botepu, we recognize that every taxpayer and business structure is unique. Therefore, we dedicate ourselves to understanding your specific financial layout and tailoring our tax solutions to meet your precise needs. Whether you are navigating provisional tax, individual returns, or corporate VAT compliance, we are committed to supporting you at every stage. Our proactive approach ensures your tax strategies evolve alongside your business or personal wealth growth. By fostering a collaborative partnership, we handle the SARS admin so you can focus on driving your success forward. We prioritize building long-term relationships, ensuring continuous, hassle-free tax compliance year after year.
